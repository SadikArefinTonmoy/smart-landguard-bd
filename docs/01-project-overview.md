# Project overview

## Problem

Land transactions in Bangladesh often involve fragmented documents, inconsistent identifiers, missing mutation history, and uncertainty around legal status. Buyers may receive partial information from sellers or ad listings and make decisions without meaningful verification.

## Solution

Smart LandGuard BD helps people evaluate a property through a structured evidence workflow. It supports:

- land search with property identifiers
- document upload and review
- ownership timeline analysis
- mutation status review
- dispute and acquisition screening
- risk assessment with explainable reasoning
- buyer guidance and transaction tracking

## Unique differentiation

The system is designed around evidence transparency rather than a fake guarantee of safety. It clearly distinguishes:

- authorized source data
- user-submitted information
- extracted document fields
- AI risk indicators
- human review requirements
- unavailable or inconclusive data

This makes the product more trustworthy and more useful in real-world decision support.
